# Student status

This section covers situations when the normal course of study changes: individual schedule, academic leave, transfer, reinstatement, or expulsion.

## Individual learning path

An individual learning path can include:

- an individual study schedule;
- an individual study plan;
- accelerated study.

These formats are needed if a student cannot follow the standard schedule or has grounds for a special order of completing the program.

## Individual study schedule

An individual schedule is a document that sets personal deadlines for completing courses and submitting work during the semester.

It exempts the student from attending classes on the general schedule, but does not exempt them from learning outcomes. Each course has checkpoints that are agreed with the instructor.

### Who can arrange it

For example, a full-time student can arrange an individual schedule if they actively participate in research, public, sports, or cultural events, and the schedule of these events conflicts with classes.

### Important conditions

- Documents must be submitted within the first month of the semester.
- The application is reviewed for up to 21 calendar days.
- For full-time students, the switch is possible only after the first interim assessment, i.e. after the first exam period.
- A repeat individual schedule is possible if the previous one was successfully completed.

To arrange it, contact the Student Office.

## Academic leave

Academic leave is a temporary break from studies for a valid reason.

Reasons can vary: medical circumstances, family circumstances, emergencies, and other grounds provided by university rules.

To arrange it, contact the Student Office and provide supporting documents.

## Expulsion

Educational relations end when a student completes their studies or is expelled early.

### Early expulsion at the student's initiative

Possible reasons:

- own request;
- transfer to another educational institution;
- termination of the paid education contract.

### Early expulsion at FEFU's initiative

Possible reasons:

- academic underperformance;
- failure to return from academic leave;
- overdue tuition payment;
- the student did not start classes;
- violation of admission procedure if it led to unlawful enrollment;
- disciplinary penalty;
- offenses or crimes related to public order and safety.

To arrange expulsion, submit an application via **Personal Account / One-Stop Service**.
