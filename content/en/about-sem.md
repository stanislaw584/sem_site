# About SEM

This section briefly explains what the FEFU School of Economics and Management is and which units may be important to a student.

## Mission and values

The mission of SEM is to build a community of responsible professionals and leaders, support their development, and contribute to the socio-economic development of the Russian Far East.

Key values:

- continuous development;
- responsible management;
- attention to stakeholder needs;
- equality and inclusion;
- internationalization;
- entrepreneurial approach;
- new technologies.

## What SEM does

SEM is a large educational unit of FEFU. The School runs programs in economics, management, services, and tourism, cooperates with Russian and international partners, and develops project work, hands-on classes, and research activity.

## How SEM is organized

The School consists of the Directorate, departments, chairs, and administrative units.

### Directorate

The School is led by the Director. Specific areas are overseen by Deputy Directors, for example for research and innovation, or for academic and student affairs.

### Departments and chairs

Academic units bring together instructors by field. SEM has:

- Department of Applied Economics;
- Department of Socio-Economic Research and Regional Development;
- Department of Finance;
- Department of Data-Driven Management;
- Department of Management and Entrepreneurship;
- Department of Marketing and Market Development;
- Department of Tourism and Hospitality;
- Affiliated Chair of Modern Banking.

### Administrative office

The administrative office helps with academic matters:

- organization of the academic process;
- transfers and reinstatements;
- changing a group or subgroup;
- conflict situations;
- student status issues;
- consultations if you do not know where to turn.

### Head of the degree program

The Head of the program is responsible for the quality and development of the degree program: curriculum, internships, final assessment, employer engagement, and feedback.

### Deputy Head of the degree program

For a student, this is one of the most important contacts. You can reach out to the Deputy Head of the program with questions about studies, consultations, individual situations, and routing.

### Student Center

The Student Center supports extracurricular activities:

- adaptation of first-year students;
- tutors;
- curators;
- events;
- student council;
- work with class monitors;
- disciplinary issues;
- SEM social media.

### Tutors and curators

Tutors help first-year students adapt. Curators hold curator hours and help the group during their first year of study.

### Class monitors (starosta)

The class monitor helps the group receive organizational information and communicate with instructors and staff. However, important issues are better double-checked through official sources: email, personal account, the FEFU website, or staff members.

### Office of Development Projects and School-wide Services

This office is in charge of projects, events, external opportunities, information resources, and SEM social media.
