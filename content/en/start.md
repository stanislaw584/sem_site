# Get started

Before classes begin, it is important to set up the main FEFU services. Through them you will check the schedule, grades, email, library, requests, and other matters.

## FEFU single sign-on account

A single sign-on account is required to log in to the university's digital services. Once activated, you will be able to use the personal account, corporate email, and other FEFU services.

### What to do

1. Open the FEFU website.
2. Go to the **Personal Account** section.
3. Click **Account Activation**.
4. Enter your full name, passport details, and personal email address.
5. Create a password.
6. Click **Activate**.
7. Save the login that appears after activation.
8. Check that you can log in to the personal account.

### Important

- Be sure to set a password during activation.
- Save your login: you will need it to access services.
- If login does not work, contact IT support or the SEM Administrative Office.

## "My Univer" personal account

The personal account is the main digital service for students. Through it you can:

- view the schedule;
- check grades and the electronic grade book;
- submit requests via the "One-Stop Service";
- file applications about studies and accommodation;
- use library services;
- access corporate email.

## FEFU corporate email

Official university communication goes through corporate email. Requests from personal email may not be processed.

### Why check your email

Email may bring:

- messages from instructors;
- notifications about academic debts;
- letters from the Student Office;
- application deadlines;
- important announcements from SEM and FEFU units.

### How to keep up with your inbox

- Install the Yandex 360 app.
- Check email at least every two days.
- Create folders: "Instructors," "Student Office," "Mailings," "Important."
- You can set up forwarding to personal email, but official replies should be sent from the corporate address.

## Electronic grade book

The electronic grade book is in your personal account at **Studies / Grade Book**.

There you can check the results of:

- exams;
- pass/fail credits;
- term papers and projects;
- internships;
- electives.

### When to check

Check the grade book after every exam period. This is important because a grade error can affect your scholarship, rating, and dormitory placement.

### If a grade is wrong

1. First, write to the instructor who conducted the assessment.
2. If you cannot resolve the issue with the instructor, submit a request via your personal account:
   **Requests / One-Stop Service / Submit official request to the university / Studies / Academic process / Grade Book / Request to change a grade**.
