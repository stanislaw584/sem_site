# Opportunities

Beyond studies, SEM and FEFU offer many opportunities for growth: scholarships, contests, research projects, volunteering, creative arts, sports, academic mobility, and careers.

## Extracurricular activities

Students can develop themselves in creative arts, sports, public activities, and student self-government.

SEM has its own groups and initiatives:

- BomWay dance cover team;
- "Lirika" vocal group;
- BLIZTEAM dance group;
- SEM Student Council;
- SEM sports events, including Spartakiada, LAPTA GAME, and FIRE DODGEBALL.

The SEM Student Council helps to:

- improve the educational process;
- develop student initiatives;
- represent students' rights and interests;
- organize events and projects.

If you do not know where to go, reach out to the SEM Student Center.

## Student olympiads

Olympiads, contests, and championships help develop professional competencies.

SEM students take part, for example, in:

- the "I Am a Professional" olympiad;
- the "Upravlyay" youth management cup;
- WorldSkills standard championships;
- other national and professional contests.

Top performers can receive:

- cash prizes;
- benefits when applying to a Master's or doctoral program;
- internships at major companies.

## Scholarships

A scholarship is a cash payment to support and motivate students.

Main types:

- state academic scholarship;
- increased state academic scholarship for achievements;
- state social scholarship;
- scholarships of the President of the Russian Federation and the Government of the Russian Federation;
- named scholarships;
- scholarships from legal entities or individuals.

The state academic scholarship is paid to state-funded students. Some competitive scholarships are also open to tuition-paying students.

If a student finds themselves in a difficult life situation, they can apply for one-time financial aid. Such aid is usually granted no more than once per semester and requires supporting documents.

## Research

SEM supports student participation in research, conferences, scholarly paper competitions, and publications.

Where to start:

1. Choose a topic that interests you.
2. Discuss it with an instructor, research advisor, or Head of the degree program.
3. Find out about conferences, roundtables, and research clubs.
4. Watch for announcements of research events.

SEM has research laboratories:

- Laboratory for Interdisciplinary Research on Safety and Risk;
- Laboratory for Data Analysis and Applied Econometric Research;
- Laboratory for Economic Research on Global Economic Relations and Processes;
- Laboratory for Modeling of Socio-Economic Processes;
- Educational and Research Laboratory of Experimental Economics and Game Theory.

Information about research clubs is published in the group "DOM KLUBOV SEM FEFU."

## Academic mobility and travel grants

Academic mobility is an opportunity to spend part of your studies, an internship, or an event at another university or country.

What participation gives you:

- experience in an international academic environment;
- development of intercultural communication;
- improvement of a foreign language;
- exposure to other countries' educational systems;
- consultations with foreign instructors;
- new career opportunities;
- new connections.

A travel grant can help cover a trip to an academic or research event outside Primorsky Krai, elsewhere in Russia, or abroad.

Events fall into:

- academic: olympiads, contests, competitions, championships;
- research: conferences, presentation of research results, research internships.

Where to look for information:

- FEFU website: **School of Economics and Management / For Students / Academic Mobility**.

Contact for SEM academic exchanges:

- Anna B. Dolgopolova;
- address: building G, room G516;
- email: `international.sem@dvfu.ru`.

## Work and career

SEM organizes events to introduce students to employers:

- strategy sessions;
- master classes;
- roundtables;
- job fairs;
- career guidance events.

At job fairs you can talk with employers, learn about internships and vacancies, and get invitations to interviews.
