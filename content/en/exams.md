# Exams and grades

An exam period (sessiya) is the time when course results are finalized. At SEM, the final grade often depends on the cumulative rating the student earns throughout the semester.

## Main interim assessment

Interim assessment comes in the following forms:

- pass/fail credit (zachet);
- graded pass/fail (zachet with a grade);
- exam.

The form of assessment is usually specified in the curriculum and in the course grading plan.

## How the rating becomes a grade

| Student's rating | Pass/fail credit | Exam |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Less than 61% | not passed | unsatisfactory |
| 61-75% | passed | satisfactory |
| 76-85% | passed | good |
| 86-100% | passed | excellent |

## Credit week

Credit week is the last week of classroom instruction in the semester. During this time, courses are usually closed if the form of assessment is:

- a pass/fail credit;
- a graded pass/fail;
- a term project;
- a course where the exam in the grading plan has zero weight.

Assessment for distributed practical training may also take place during credit week.

## Exam period

The exam period takes place after classroom instruction. It is needed for courses that include an exam with non-zero weight.

If there is an exam, a separate day is scheduled on which the student receives the final grade for the course.

## How to find out exam period dates

Check:

- the academic calendar;
- the schedule on the FEFU website;
- corporate email;
- messages from instructors;
- information from the Deputy Head of the degree program.

The academic calendar may use codes, for example:

- `Э` (E) - exam period;
- other codes for study, practical training, and vacation periods.

## What you must do after the exam period

1. Open your personal account.
2. Check the **Rating** tab.
3. Check the **Grade Book** tab.
4. Make sure all final grades are displayed correctly.

Important: the grade must appear not only in "Rating" but also in the "Grade Book."

## If a grade is wrong

1. First contact the instructor.
2. If the issue is not resolved, submit a request through your personal account:
   **Requests / One-Stop Service / Submit official request to the university / Studies / Academic process / Grade Book / Request to change a grade**.
3. Do not delay: errors are best fixed right after the exam period.

## Individual exam deadlines

Individual deadlines may be needed if a student cannot complete assessments within the standard timeframe for a valid reason.

To arrange this, contact the Student Office through the "One-Stop Service." Prepare in advance the documents that confirm the reason.

## Retakes and academic debts

An academic debt occurs when a student does not pass the assessment for a course.

Retakes follow an approved schedule. The schedule is published on the FEFU website under **Studies / Clearing Academic Debts**.

The student is responsible for:

- checking the retake schedule;
- preparing;
- showing up at the appointed time;
- clarifying unclear points in advance with the instructor or the Deputy Head of the program.

## Additional subject commissions

If a debt is not cleared in the standard procedure, an additional subject commission may be appointed. The details depend on the specific situation and university rules.

If you do not understand what stage of retake you are at, contact the Deputy Head of the degree program or the Student Office.

## Dispute resolution commission

If a student disagrees with the way an assessment or grade has been handled, they can file an application with the dispute resolution commission.

The application is submitted through the personal account in the **One-Stop Service** section. The application should describe the substance of the dispute and include materials related to the issue.

A decision is made within 30 calendar days of receiving the application.

## SEM's main recommendations

- Check your grades after every semester.
- Do not ignore emails from the university.
- If a debt appears, find out the retake procedure right away.
- Do not wait until the last day to submit applications.
- If you do not understand the retake schedule, ask for a consultation.
- Follow university discipline and rules: violations may lead to penalties.
