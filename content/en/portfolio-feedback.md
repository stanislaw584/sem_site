# Portfolio and feedback

## Student portfolio

A portfolio is a list of your achievements during your studies. It is worth keeping from your first year.

A portfolio may be useful:

- when transferring from tuition-paying to state-funded study;
- for a reference letter for military training;
- for employment;
- when applying to a Master's or doctoral program;
- for a recommendation letter;
- for internships and contests.

## What counts in a portfolio

Usually counted are:

- GPA;
- participation in research events;
- publications;
- volunteering;
- public activities;
- sports achievements;
- creative achievements;
- discipline and adherence to rules.

Important: when preparing a reference letter, the university considers not only achievements, but also rule violations, dormitory issues, late assessments, and other disciplinary matters.

## How to get a reference letter

1. Submit a request in your personal account via **One-Stop Service**.
2. Attach documents that confirm achievements.
3. If you need the reference in a specific form, indicate this in the request.

The reference letter is prepared within 7 business days.

You can also get a recommendation letter from the Head of the degree program or a leading instructor if you need it for a Master's, doctoral program, internship, or contest.

## Student surveys

SEM runs surveys to improve the quality of education. Responses are collected in aggregated form, without access to students' personal data.

Take part in surveys when you receive them: this is a way to report problems and suggest improvements.
