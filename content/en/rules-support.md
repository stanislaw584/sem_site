# Rules and support

This section explains the university's basic rules and shows where to turn for support.

## Equality and diversity

FEFU and SEM uphold respect toward students from different cultures, countries, and languages.

SEM has many international students. In 2024, the School had more than 3,200 students, of whom 1,212 were international. They came from 19 countries across East, Southeast, Southwest, and Central Asia, Latin America, Africa, and Eastern Europe.

SEM provides support for international students:

- special academic groups for adaptation;
- instructors who work with international students;
- Master's programs in English;
- tutors in the first semester;
- mentors from FEFU's Department for International Activities Support.

For questions concerning international students, including documents for entry to the Russian Federation, contact the International Student Office: `interadvising@dvfu.ru`.

## Ethical conduct

At the university, it is important to show respect for other students, instructors, and staff.

A student is expected to:

- respect the traditions and cultural values of others;
- communicate politely and without aggression;
- avoid insults and obscene language;
- not disrupt classes;
- treat university property with care;
- keep classrooms, cafeterias, gyms, and dormitories clean;
- follow the internal rules and dormitory regulations.

In classes and at official events, clothing appropriate to an academic and business setting is recommended.

## Consequences of violations

For breaking the rules, a student may be summoned to a conflict commission. Possible consequences:

- warning;
- reprimand;
- formal reprimand;
- eviction from the dormitory;
- expulsion.

Detailed rules are posted on the FEFU website under **About the Educational Organization / Documents**. Dormitory rules are in the campus and accommodation section.

## Inclusive education

FEFU follows the principles of inclusive education. The university accepts students with disabilities and various health limitations.

The Russky Island campus is equipped for a barrier-free environment:

- ramps;
- widened entrances;
- wide corridors and passages;
- accessible restrooms;
- equipment in the library, including Braille displays, a reading machine, an electronic magnifier, and other aids.

## Academic integrity and plagiarism

Academic integrity means that a student completes coursework independently and honestly cites sources.

Plagiarism is the use of someone else's ideas or text without crediting the author. This is a breach of research ethics.

At FEFU, term papers and graduation theses undergo mandatory plagiarism checks.

To avoid problems:

- do not copy someone else's text without citation;
- format your sources properly;
- do not submit another person's work as your own;
- check citation requirements with your instructor.

## Tutoring and mentoring

In the first semester, a group may have a tutor — an upper-year student. They help with adaptation, answer questions, and share their experience.

During the first year, an instructor-curator may also work with the group. They help students get to know the university and their future professional field.

## Deputy Head of the degree program

Throughout their studies, a student can reach out to the Deputy Head of their degree program.

You can turn to them if:

- you are having academic difficulties;
- you do not know who to contact;
- you need advice about your educational path;
- a conflict or difficult situation has arisen;
- you have questions about development opportunities.
