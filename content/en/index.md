# International Student Guide to SEM

This section turns the large SEM handbook into clear pages for the website. The information is grouped not by the formal structure of the document, but by student tasks: how to start studying, where to find your schedule, what to do about grades, where to turn for help, and what opportunities the university offers.

## Main sections

1. [Get started](start.md)  
   Personal account, corporate email, electronic grade book, account activation.

2. [Everyday studies](study.md)  
   Curriculum, schedule, rating, instructors, library, teamwork.

3. [Exams and grades](exams.md)  
   Credits, exams, retakes, individual deadlines, grade errors.

4. [Rules and support](rules-support.md)  
   Conduct, academic integrity, inclusion, support for international students, psychological help.

5. [Student status](student-status.md)  
   Individual schedule, academic leave, expulsion.

6. [Opportunities](opportunities.md)  
   Scholarships, olympiads, contests, volunteering, research, mobility, careers.

7. [Portfolio and feedback](portfolio-feedback.md)  
   Reference letters, recommendation letters, SEM website and social media, surveys.

8. [Contacts](contacts.md)  
   Whom to contact for studies, documents, visa, dormitory, IT support, and emergencies.

9. [About SEM](about-sem.md)  
   A brief overview of the School, its degree programs, departments, and student support.
