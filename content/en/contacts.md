# Contacts

If you do not know where to turn, start with the SEM Administrative Office or the Student Office. They will help route your question to the right unit.

## Quick routing

| Question | Where to go |
| --- | --- |
| Studies, schedule, groups, academic conflict | SEM Administrative Office or Deputy Head of the program |
| Applications, certificates, individual schedule, academic leave, expulsion | Student Office / One-Stop Service |
| Visa, registration, international students | International Student Office, Visa and Registration Office |
| Dormitory and accommodation | Accommodation and Hospitality Office / dormitories |
| Personal account, email, Wi-Fi, single sign-on | IT support |
| Scholarships | Department of Scholarship Programs |
| Extracurricular activities | SEM Student Center |
| Contests, academic mobility, travel grants | Office of Development Projects and School-wide Services |
| Psychological help | Situation Center, clinic, helplines |
| Security, passes | Department of Integrated Security |

## FEFU: Student Office

The Student Office operates as a "One-Stop Service." Through it you can:

- submit an application for academic leave or expulsion;
- submit an application for course credit transfer;
- arrange individual assessment deadlines;
- arrange an individual study plan or schedule;
- submit a transfer application;
- update personal data;
- obtain a certificate of enrollment;
- clarify academic performance questions.

Applications can be submitted remotely via **Personal Account / Requests / One-Stop Service**.

Contacts:

- address: Russky Island, FEFU Campus, building E, level 6, section 5, E 639;
- landmark: the "puck" (round building), entrance from the street side of building F;
- phone: `8 (423) 265 24 24`, ext. `2164`, `2118`;
- email: `documents@dvfu.ru`.
